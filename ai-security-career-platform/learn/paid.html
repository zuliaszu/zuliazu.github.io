Paid courses and certifications

7 of 48 postings name any certification, and in most of those it is listed as a plus, not a requirement. Free material covers the skills; what paid options add is structure, graded labs and a line on a CV that some screeners look for.

Costs, durations and prerequisites are copied from each provider's own page on the research date; "not stated" means the page did not say. Prices change; check before you pay.
